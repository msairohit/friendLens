# 📸 FriendLens

### *Because the best recommendations come from the people who know you best!*

Welcome to **FriendLens**! 🌟 Are you tired of scrolling through endless star ratings from complete strangers, wondering if that "5-star masterpiece" is actually just paid hype? Or maybe you're stuck in the dreaded loop of *“What should we watch tonight?”* 

FriendLens is here to change that. It is a social review and recommendation platform built entirely around **trust, friendship, and privacy**. Instead of relying on random internet review scores, FriendLens focuses on the opinions of the people you actually know and trust: **your friends!**

---

## ✨ Key Features You’ll Love

### 🤝 1. Trusted Reviews from Real Friends
No bots. No paid reviews. No sponsored clout. FriendLens creates a personalized feed showing you reviews exclusively from your friends and your friends-of-friends (your trusted network). You get honest, raw, and authentic opinions from the people whose tastes you actually understand.

### 🎬 2. Movies, TV Shows... and Beyond!
Right now, FriendLens is your ultimate entertainment companion, featuring full support for **Movies and TV Shows**. But this is just the beginning! Soon, you'll be able to rate and discover everything in your lifestyle:
*   🏨 Hotels & Staycations
*   🍕 Restaurants & Cafés
*   🛍️ Malls & Shopping Spots
*   📚 Books, Games, and more!

### 🍿 3. The Ultimate "What to Watch Next" Guide
Say goodbye to decision paralysis! Simply check what your network is watching and loving. You can filter recommendations to see what’s popular specifically among your friends, giving you a custom shortlist tailored to your social circle's taste.

### 🔍 4. Quick Contact Search
Want to find your real-world friends on FriendLens? Our contact search makes it super simple! Scan your phone contacts to instantly see who is already on the platform, and connect with them in a single tap.

### ➕ 5. Connect and Grow Your Circle
Easily send and receive friend requests. Build your secure network, see pending requests, and manage your connections all from a clean, intuitive dashboard.

### 🔒 6. Privacy First: Multiple Levels of Sharing
We believe your privacy is yours to control. When you write a review, you decide exactly who gets to see it with multiple sharing levels:
*   🌎 **Global:** Share with the whole world and contribute to the global feed.
*   👥 **Network Only:** Share with just your friends and their friends.
*   🕵️ **Anonymous/Private:** Share your rating while keeping your identity fully protected.

### ⚡ 7. Seamless Movie/TV Details (TMDb Integration)
No need to type out release dates, genres, or look for poster images. Thanks to our seamless TMDb integration, simply search for a movie or show, and FriendLens will automatically pull in high-resolution posters, official descriptions, and details for you.

### ⭐ 8. Speak Your Mind & Add Ratings
Add your personal ratings, write down your thoughts, and share your experiences! The clean, beautiful interface makes it incredibly fast and fun to log your ratings.

### 🎨 9. Beautiful Themes
Make the app truly yours! Match your style or environment by switching between gorgeous, eye-catching themes designed to look premium and feel incredibly smooth.

---

## 🚀 Get Started

1.  **Create your account** in seconds.
2.  **Find your friends** via Contact Search or User ID.
3.  **Log your first review** and see what your friends are saying!

*Happy reviewing, and welcome to your new lens on recommendations!* 👓✨
